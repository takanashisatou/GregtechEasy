# GTECore Multiblock Machine Encyclopedia

GTECore addresses the tedious pipeline stacking in the mid-to-late tech progression by designing a large number of multiblock machines that combine **ultra-high parallel processing capability** with **aggregated production logic**.

## ⚡ Super Electric Industrial Multiblocks

Entering the electric age, GTECore provides all-in-one and advanced processing centers:

```mermaid

    A[Raw materials / Crude ore / Chemicals] --> B[Component Factory / Circuit Factory]
    A --> C[Ore Comprehensive Processing Center 7 modes]
    A --> D[Integrated Petrochemical Plant / Desulfurization Tower]
    B & C & D --> E[Miracle Ring 1B parallel no research]
    E --> F[Ultimate tech products and superstring/Yin-Yang circuits]
```

### 1. Core Production Factories

- **§6 Component Factory (`gtceu:component_factory`)**:
  - **Positioning**: Produces motors, pumps, pistons, robot arms, conveyor belts, LEDs, and other common basic components in one step.
  - **Features**: Directly skips tedious intermediate sub-processes, rapidly producing standard industrial parts of the specified voltage tier.
- **§6 Circuit Factory (`gtceu:circuit_factory`)**:
  - **Positioning**: Integrates circuit substrate, chip etching, and integrated packaging.
  - **Features**: Supports cross-recipe parallel hatches, fully accelerating circuit board production across the entire voltage gradient from ULV to MAX.
- **§6 Miracle Ring (`gtceu:miracle_ring`)**:
  - **Positioning**: The ultimate assembly facility for industrial miracles.
  - **Features**: Possesses **1 billion (1B) parallel** and **1t Subtick overclocking**, and can directly run assembly line recipes **without any research/assembly line research**!
- **Chemistry Terminator (`gtecore:chemistry_terminator`)**:
  - **Positioning**: "Subverts the existence of chemistry and physics, representing the end of chemistry."
  - **Features**: Aggregates complex multi-step long-chain chemical reactions into one click, rapidly synthesizing various ultimate polymers and acid media.
- **Ten-in-One Universal Processing Factory (`gtecore:ten_in_one`)**:
  - **Positioning**: A universal integrated box that combines 10 basic processes including centrifugation, electrolysis, chemical leaching, polymerization, and high-pressure reaction.

### 2. Ore and Fluid Refining System

- **§6 Ore Comprehensive Processing Center (`gtecore:ore_process_center`)**:
  - Supports **7 programming circuit modes**, achieving 5-8x ore refining with different product orientations (fully integrated crushing, washing, thermal separation, centrifugation, and electromagnetic beneficiation), supports 1t Subtick overclocking.
- **Integrated Petrochemical Plant (`gtecore:integrated_petrochemical_plant`)**:
  - Integrates the entire chain of crude oil fractionation, catalytic cracking, reforming, and desulfurization, producing all hydrocarbon light gases and aromatics in a single machine.
- **Desulfurization Machine (`gtceu:desulfurization`)**:
  - Quickly purifies various heavy sulfur-containing fuels, recovering high-purity sulfur powder as a byproduct.
- **Simple/Advanced Fluid Drilling Rig (`gtecore:easy_fluid_drilling_rig` / `not_hard_fluid_drilling_rig`)**:
  - Automatically extracts bedrock fluid veins, never depleting, without complex pipeline exploration.

### 3. High-End and Superconducting Cable Processing

- **§6 Wire Factory (`gtecore:wiremill_factory`)**: One-click production of all metal single, double, quadruple, octuple, and sixteen-fold wires and superconducting cables.
- **§6 Crystal Center (`gtecore:crystal_center`)**: Large-scale automated cultivation of monocrystalline silicon columns, emeralds, sapphires, and charged Certus quartz crystals.
- **§6 Quantum Cable Assembler (`gtecore:quantum_cable_assembler`)**: Specifically for high-speed manufacturing of quantum fiber optics and hyper-dimensional energy transmission cables.
- **§3 Starblade Etching Machine (`gtecore:starblade_etching_machine`)**: Uses high-energy beams in the extreme ultraviolet/X-ray band to etch galaxy-scale micro-nano chips.
